The Three Research Objectives

Phase 1: Completed
🛡️

1. Intelligent Security-to-Isolation Mapping

To detect 5G network threats in real time and dynamically map compromised slices to L0/L1/L2 isolation escalation without SLA downgrades.

Phase 2: Proposed
🧠

2. Learning-Based Admission Control

To formulate a reinforcement learning policy predicting downstream network impact to dynamically admit, reject, or defer slices for profit optimization.

Phase 3: Proposed
⚡

3. Intelligent Algorithm Selection

To construct an intelligent meta-classifier selecting between Exact (SCIP) and Heuristic solvers to satisfy sub-second slice deadlines.

Objective 1: System Formulation & Architecture

  • Telemetry & Threat Detection Trained a 300-tree Random Forest classifier on 5G packet telemetry, achieving 100% test accuracy on held-out attack patterns.
  • Risk Aggregation Formulated a robust slice-level risk score that continuously aggregates packet-level attack probabilities over time.
  • Dynamic Isolation Engine Engineered an SLA-safe decision engine that escalates risky slices to L1, L2, or Quarantine while mathematically guaranteeing the customer's base SLA is never downgraded.
  • Surrogate ML Optimization Evaluated 165 candidate threshold policies and trained an ML regression surrogate to discover the exact Pareto-optimal thresholds (0.30, 0.35, 0.90) that maximize profit while maintaining strict exposure limits.

Real-World Impact: How This Research Changes the Field & the World

SecGuard bridges the decades-old divide between deep cyber-threat telemetry and carrier-grade optimization, unlocking unprecedented safety and profitability for the global 5G/6G ecosystem.

1. Transforming the Telecommunications & Networking Field

🛡️

Autonomous Self-Defending Slicing

Overturns the conventional static paradigm where isolation is frozen at slice creation. SecGuard introduces a living, closed-loop immune system that continuously inspects packet flows and dynamically isolates active threats in milliseconds.

📈

Breaking the Profit vs. Risk Deadlock

Telecom operators historically refused strict isolation because over-provisioning dedicated hardware destroyed profit margins by over 65%. Statistical validation demonstrates that ML threshold optimization recovers +164.9% profit, proving carrier-grade security is economically lucrative.

🔬

Fulfilling the Open IEEE Research Challenge

Directly solves the critical challenge left open in IEEE Transactions on Mobile Computing (Bolourian et al., 2026): establishing the first mathematically sound bridge between machine learning intrusion detection and mixed-integer slice optimization.

2. Changing the World: Protecting Mission-Critical Digital Society

🏥

Protecting Human Lives in Critical Services

In 5G/6G, a botnet attacking a smart-fridge or traffic sensor could crash a shared server hosting remote robotic surgeries, connected ambulances, or autonomous vehicle (V2X) corridors. SecGuard autonomously isolates infected nodes without taking life-critical services offline.

🌐

Unlocking Global Enterprise 5G/6G Adoption

Removes the #1 fear preventing defense sectors, smart factories, smart grids, and healthcare providers from adopting public multi-tenant 5G—delivering provable zero-trust isolation and SLA guarantees without prohibitive dedicated infrastructure costs.

🌱

Green, Sustainable Cloud-RAN Computing

Replaces the wasteful industry habit of permanently running high-power dedicated physical servers (L2) with intelligent, risk-proportional scaling. This slashes idle server power draw and reduces the carbon footprint of telecom edge clouds worldwide.
